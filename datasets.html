Datasets

Open datasets I have built or contributed to for research on irrigation, agriculture, water resources, and food security. All are free to use for research and operational work.

Irrigation

MIRCA-OS

Monthly irrigated and rainfed cropped areas worldwide through the 21st century.

Download from HydroShare or read the Scientific Data paper

GMIA-NEXT

Next-generation global map of irrigated areas at 30 m resolution, with irrigation probability and binary irrigated/non-irrigated maps for the 2023/24 growing season.

Download from Zenodo or read the preprint

Area Equipped for Irrigation (AEI)

Annual global extent of area equipped for irrigation, from 2000 onward.

Download from Zenodo

Irrigation expansion around new dams

Accompanies the study Have irrigated areas actually expanded around new irrigation dams? and supports analysis of irrigation change near newly built irrigation dams worldwide.

Download from Zenodo

Crop statistics

HarvestStat Africa

Harmonized subnational crop production, harvested area, and yield statistics across Sub-Saharan Africa.

View on GitHub or read the Scientific Data paper
